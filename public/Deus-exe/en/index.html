Privacy Policy

GGemCo (“we,” “us,” or the “Company”) respects the privacy of users of the mobile game Deus.exe (package name: com.ggemco.deus, the “Game”) and processes personal data transparently and securely in accordance with applicable law.

This Policy applies to Google Analytics for Firebase (GA4) used by the Game and to optional external surveys that a user may choose to open.

1. Data We Process and How We Collect It

The Game does not require registration or login and does not ask users to enter direct identifiers such as their name, telephone number, or address in the Game. When the Game runs, however, the Firebase SDK may automatically generate or collect the following data:

The Game currently does not use Firebase Authentication, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Performance Monitoring, Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, or Unity IAP for real-money payments. In-game item-purchase and virtual-currency events are gameplay analytics and do not represent real-money transactions.

The Game does not set a Company-created account ID, email address, or user name as a GA4 User-ID.

2. Purposes of Processing

We process the data described above to:

  • Measure users, sessions, and general Game usage;
  • Analyze progression and drop-off in tutorials, quests, maps, skills, and boss encounters;
  • Improve game difficulty, balance, content, and user experience;
  • Identify abnormally duplicated or missing analytics events; and
  • Evaluate service operation plans and the effects of updates.

We do not use this data to display personalized advertising, and the Game currently contains no advertising SDK. If we later introduce advertising, login, real-money payments, crash reporting, or new analytics tools, we will update this Policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure before or when the change takes effect.

3. Retention

User-level event data in GA4 is retained for the period configured by us in the Google Analytics property. A standard GA4 property allows an event-data retention period of 2 or 14 months, and we select the shortest period reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy. When the configured period expires, event-level data is deleted through Google’s regular deletion process.

Standard aggregated GA4 reports may not be affected by the event-data retention setting. We do not separately retain aggregated reports beyond the period reasonably necessary for internal operations. If applicable law requires retention, we retain only the required data for the legally required period.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not sell personal data. We do not disclose personal data to third parties outside the purposes described in this Policy unless the user separately consents or applicable law requires or permits the disclosure.

Google’s processing to provide Google Analytics is described below as service-provider processing and an international transfer.

7. Optional External Survey

At a demo completion screen or similar point, the Game may allow a user to choose to open a Google Forms survey in an external browser. Merely opening the link does not cause us to collect survey answers inside the Game. Survey responses are processed only if the user completes and submits the form.

The Korean and English surveys are not configured to request or collect direct identifiers such as a name, email address, telephone number, or account information. We use anonymous survey responses only to improve the Game and analyze user feedback. Users should not enter their own or another person’s personal data in a free-text response. Google’s Privacy Policy applies to information that Google independently processes when a user accesses or uses Google Forms.

8. Deletion

We delete personal data without undue delay when its purpose has been fulfilled or its retention period expires. Electronic files are deleted using methods designed to prevent practical recovery or restoration. Data stored in GA4 is deleted using Google Analytics retention and data-deletion controls. Data retained because of a legal obligation is kept separately and deleted when that period ends.

9. User Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or withdrawal of consent. Requests may be submitted using the contact information in Section 10, and we will respond without undue delay after completing any reasonably necessary verification.

Because the Game does not use member accounts or a Company-assigned User-ID, there may be technical limits on locating or deleting GA4 app-instance data associated with a particular individual. Users can limit additional collection by:

  • Disabling the privacy/analytics option in versions of the Game that provide such a setting;
  • Deleting or resetting the Android Advertising ID and changing ads privacy settings in Android; and
  • Uninstalling the Game to delete locally stored Game data from the device.

Uninstalling the Game does not automatically delete GA4 reports that have already been transmitted and aggregated. Please contact us under Section 10 to ask whether an individual request can be fulfilled.

11. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not designed for children under the age of 14. If we learn that we processed personal data of a child under 14 without verifiable consent from a legal representative, we will delete it without undue delay as required by applicable law. 12. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to prevent loss, theft, unauthorized disclosure, alteration, or destruction of personal data. These measures include access controls, encryption in transit using TLS, protection of administrative accounts, data minimization, and periodic review.

13. Changes to This Policy

If this Policy changes, we will provide notice before the effective date through an in-Game notice, the distribution page, or our website. If a change materially affects user rights, including a change to the categories collected, purposes, or disclosures, we will provide separate notice or obtain consent where required by law.
